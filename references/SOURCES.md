# Discover editorial studies

These independent studies demonstrate a completed brandbun guide. They are not guides published or endorsed by the featured organisations. They use original source artwork, not recreated logos. Rights to marks and artwork stay with their respective owners. This collection does not grant permission to reuse third-party marks or media.

`manifest.json` records each exact source URL, byte size, dimensions where applicable, and SHA-256. Images and fonts are bundled locally, so reading a study does not hotlink third-party files. Source guidelines can change; recheck them before updating a study.

## NASA

- Brand guidelines and digital/print typography: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/brand-guidelines/
- Original vector insignia: the NASA website's own SVG, listed in the manifest. It is not the 3D-printable insignia render or a recreated logo.
- Insignia elements diagram: official brand guideline image.
- Inter and Public Sans: NASA's website webfont files. These are the digital typography, separate from its Helvetica/Garamond print guidance. Their SIL OFL licences are included separately.
- Cosmic Cliffs (NGC 3324), Webb NIRCam image: https://science.nasa.gov/asset/webb/cosmic-cliffs-in-the-carina-nebula-nircam-image/
- Image credit: NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI. Bundled image is NASA's 1280px rendition; the source link provides the full-resolution original.
- Media/mark restrictions: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

Editorial descriptions and type-specimen sentences are written for this study; they are not official brand slogans. Colour selections are excerpts, not complete specifications. NASA marks are restricted and must not imply endorsement. Consult each original guide before production use.

## Spotify

- Source: https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design
- Full and icon logo packs: the 2024 RGB ZIP files linked directly by that page; selected SVGs are bundled unchanged.
- Clear-space diagram: original SVG from the same guidance page.
- This study covers partner/integration guidance. Its typography section follows the documented platform-font recommendation, using Arial. It does not claim that Arial is Spotify's proprietary corporate typeface.

## Slack

- Current public media kit: https://slack.com/media-kit
- Original octothorpe SVG: Slack website navigation asset, recorded in the manifest. Low-resolution navigation wordmark PNGs were excluded.
- Product and illustration images: original media-kit preview images, credited to Slack. These are source examples, not a representation of the latest product UI.
- Palette and recorded typefaces: https://a.slack-edge.com/4d5bb/marketing/img/media-kit/slack_brand_guidelines_september2020.pdf (pages 24, 27–28). The dated source is identified in the guide. The PDF is linked, not redistributed.
- Larsseit and Slack Circular are recorded as brand typefaces; they are not bundled. All comparison specimens explicitly identify Arial. No proprietary font was copied.
- Usage terms: https://slack.com/terms-of-service/slack-brand
