Small, simple, independent.

brandbun is made by Leo Lune. These are the practical details of using it.

Your library

Free accounts hold 5 brands and 20 MB. Early supporter starts at $2 USD, paid once, for 25 brands and 100 MB. Every support amount receives the same upgrade. Files are limited to 5 MB. There is no recurring subscription and no promise of unlimited storage.

Your upgrade is linked to your sign-in account and stays with that account while brandbun operates. A one-time upgrade is not a guarantee that the service will operate forever. Save a downloaded backup of important work.

Your work and sharing

You keep ownership of your content and must have permission to upload and share it. Invitation recipients can view and download the guide you share. Keep private material out of publicly shared presentations.

Saving and recovery

Guest work stays in the browser where you create it. Sign in and save it to your email for cloud storage. Download library backup creates a separate recovery copy. Automatic service backups are currently disabled. Undo for deleted brands and assets is available for 15 seconds while the page stays open.

Help, payments, and account deletion

Contact leolunelove@gmail.com for a payment problem, refund request, extra space, or account deletion. Please use your account email so ownership can be verified. Account deletion is handled manually after verification; a request does not delete anything immediately. Necessary payment records may be retained as described in the privacy policy.
