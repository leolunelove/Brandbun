Your work, your privacy.

Privacy policy · Updated 14 October 2026

brandbun is a tool for creating, saving, and sharing brand guides. It is operated by Leo Lune. This page explains the information used to provide those features.

Saving your library

Before sign-in, guides are stored in your browser. When you sign in with your email and save your library, brandbun stores your email, account identifier, guide content, uploaded assets, and storage usage in Supabase. Supabase Auth manages your sign-in session; Resend delivers sign-in emails through Supabase. Your browser keeps a session so you can remain signed in.

Your cloud library is private unless you share a guide. A recipient who accepts an invitation can view and download that guide. Backups restored into brandbun become new copies and do not carry invitation permissions.

Supporter payments

Stripe handles the hosted checkout. Your verified sign-in email and account reference are used to link a purchase to your library. brandbun stores the payment reference and upgrade status; it does not receive or store your full card details. Stripe processes payment information under its own privacy policy. A sandbox checkout is a test and does not charge money or grant a live upgrade.

Contact messages

The contact form sends your reply email, topic, and message to Leo through Supabase Edge Functions and Resend. Messages are retained in the support inbox to respond and resolve your request. Hashed abuse-prevention records are retained for up to two days. Do not include passwords or payment card details.

Retention and your choices

Your saved cloud library remains available across visits. You can edit or delete your guides, download a recovery copy, sign out. Deleted guides and assets may remain temporarily in infrastructure recovery copies or operational logs. Payment records may be retained where needed for accounting or resolving a payment issue.

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Hosting, authentication, storage, email, and payment providers may process technical records such as request times, IP addresses, and errors to operate and protect their services. brandbun does not add advertising trackers.

For a copy of your account information, an account deletion request, or a privacy question, contact leolunelove@gmail.com. Keep downloaded backups private: they contain the guides and files you saved.
